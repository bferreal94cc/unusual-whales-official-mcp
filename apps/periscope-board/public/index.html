Tide

Market tide

Tide is cumulative net premium through the session — every trade lifted at the ask adds, every trade hit on the bid subtracts. The curve's shape matters more than its endpoint: a line that rolls over mid-session says buyers stopped paying up.

Gamma

Gamma exposure

Kept separate from flow on purpose. Flow is what traded; gamma exposure is the inventory dealers are left holding, and it predicts how they hedge. Negative net gamma means hedging amplifies moves rather than damping them. Blue is positive gamma throughout this board, red negative — colour never means anything else here.
